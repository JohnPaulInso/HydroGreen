Pest & Leaf Health Diagnostic Guide

Quick reference for identifying nutrient deficiencies, tipburn, and pest issues.

🍃 Nitrogen Deficiency

Lower leaves turn pale yellow while upper leaves stay light green. Increase SNAP EC concentration slightly.

🍂 Tipburn (Calcium / Heat)

Leaf tips turn brown and crispy. Caused by high humidity or midday heat. Increase airflow with a fan.

🧪 Iron Chlorosis (pH High)

Young leaves show yellowing between green veins. pH is above 6.8 — add pH Down to drop to 6.0.

🦟 Aphids / Thrips

Tiny yellow spots or curled leaves. Spray with mild neem oil or dilute soap solution early morning.
